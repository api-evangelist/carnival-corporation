Carnival Corporation won the Community Contribution Leader Award at the 2026 ESG Shipping Awards in Athens for its work at Celebration Key, a $600 million Grand Bahama destination. The project has generated over 1,000 permanent local jobs, with 99% of hires Bahamian, and is projected to contribute $9.7 billion to The Bahamas' GDP over two decades.
