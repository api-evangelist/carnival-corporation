Princess Cruises and Holland America Line were both awarded Blue Circle honors under the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority's EcoAction Program, recognizing fleetwide sustainability initiatives that cut air emissions and underwater noise. The two lines have now earned a combined 28 Blue Circle awards.
