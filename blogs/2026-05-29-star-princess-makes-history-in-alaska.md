Star Princess became Carnival Corporation's first LNG-powered ship to operate in Alaska, sailing seven-day Inside Passage itineraries through mid-September. Princess Cruises is also investing in local communities along the route, supporting veterans services, marine conservation, childcare, and trail preservation.
