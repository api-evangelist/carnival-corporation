After two weeks in dry dock in Rotterdam, AIDAprima returned to service with refreshed public spaces, over 150,000 square feet of new recycled carpet, updated cabins, and a new underwater hull coating to reduce fuel consumption. The Carnival Maritime shipyard team completed the refit in 14 days.
