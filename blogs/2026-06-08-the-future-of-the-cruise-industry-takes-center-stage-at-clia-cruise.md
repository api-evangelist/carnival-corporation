At the CLIA UK Cruise Summit, Carnival Corporation's Richard Cooke joined industry leaders on a panel about decarbonization, covering shore power expansion and future fuels. The discussion emphasized alignment between ports, policymakers, and operators to advance the cruise industry's sustainability goals.
