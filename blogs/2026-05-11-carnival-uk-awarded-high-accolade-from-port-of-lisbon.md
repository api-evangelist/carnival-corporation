Carnival UK & P&O Cruises president Paul Ludlow accepted the Excellence Award in Community Engagement from the Port of Lisbon at Seatrade Cruise Global in Miami, recognizing donations from ships' fleets and beach cleans that collected over 70 kilograms of trash near Lisbon.
