The Port of Seattle honored Carnival Corporation and Carnival Cruise Line with Sustainable Century Awards. Carnival Corporation won for its Quiet Sound program participation to protect orcas, while Carnival Cruise Line's "Our Sea Pledge" program cut food waste and recycled over 25 million pounds of material in 2025.
